StudyPack: Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026. Published by StudyPack. Contact: studypack.help@gmail.com.

In short: StudyPack has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. The developer never receives your course materials, your study pages or your API keys. They stay in your browser, and your course materials go only to the AI service you choose.

What StudyPack accesses, and why

  • Your Canvas course content. When you press Start on a course, StudyPack reads that course through the Canvas API using your existing Canvas login: the module list, pages, assignment and quiz instructions, discussions, the syllabus, calendar events and course files. It uses these only to build your study page.
  • Your open tabs' addresses and titles. StudyPack looks at these on your computer to find your Canvas course tab and your chatbot tab. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your chatbot tab (chatbot mode). Unless you turn automatic typing off in Settings, StudyPack types its requests into the chatbot tab you chose and reads the replies. With it off, you copy and paste each request yourself and StudyPack doesn't touch the chatbot site.
  • API keys (API mode). If you enter an API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, StudyPack stores it in your browser to make requests for you.
  • Questions you ask the tutor. When you use "Ask the tutor" or a quiz hint, your question and the matching passages from your course materials are sent to the AI the tutor is using (see below). Your tutor conversation is saved with the study page in your browser.

StudyPack only runs on sites you grant it access to through Chrome's permission prompt, and only while it's building a study page you started.

Where your data goes

  • Stored in your browser only. Study pages, the course text behind each one (so the tutor can answer questions), tutor conversations, settings, API keys and in-progress builds are kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.
  • Chrome's built-in AI stays on your computer. If your computer has Chrome's on-device AI (Gemini Nano), the tutor and quiz hints may use it; then your question and passages are processed on your device and not sent anywhere.
  • Sent to the AI service you choose, and nowhere else. To write your study page, the text of your course materials is sent either
    • to the chatbot website you're signed in to (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), where it becomes part of a conversation in your own account; or
    • directly from your browser to the API of the provider you selected (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), using your own API key.
    That service handles the data under its own privacy policy and terms. See Anthropic, OpenAI and Google, or the policy of whichever chatbot you use. Free tiers of some services may use what you send to improve their products. By default StudyPack uses Google's Gemini API with your own free key, and on Google's free tier Google may use what you send, including your course materials, to improve its products (see the Gemini API terms). If you don't want that, use a paid key or your own chatbot tab instead.
  • Never to the developer. StudyPack has no server and sends nothing to its developer: no course content, no usage data, no analytics, no crash reports.

What StudyPack doesn't do

  • It doesn't sell or share your data, and doesn't use it for advertising.
  • It doesn't use or transfer your data for anything other than building your study pages, and never to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • It doesn't track your browsing or read sites you haven't granted it access to.

StudyPack's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Study pages stay until you delete them. You can delete one from its page, or everything (study pages, settings and API keys) in StudyPack's Settings under Your data, which can also remove StudyPack's access to Canvas and chatbot sites.
  • You can have study pages deleted automatically 1, 7 or 30 days after the exam. Unfinished builds are cleared after a day.
  • Uninstalling StudyPack removes all of its stored data from your browser.
  • StudyPack can't delete copies held elsewhere: conversations in your chatbot account (delete those in the chatbot), study pages you downloaded as files, or anything you shared yourself.

Security

Data is stored in Chrome's extension storage, which other websites and extensions can't read. API keys are stored there in plain form, like other browser-stored credentials, so don't install StudyPack on a computer you share with people you don't trust. All network requests use HTTPS.

Children

StudyPack is meant for college and high-school students and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your responsibilities

Use StudyPack only with course materials you're allowed to access, and follow your school's policies and the terms of the AI service you choose.

Changes to this policy

If StudyPack's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date, and the change will be described in the extension's update notes before it takes effect.
