StudyPack

Turn your Canvas course into a study page for your next exam.

Open your course's Modules page, click StudyPack, type the exam name and press Start. StudyPack figures out what the exam covers, reads your course files, and builds:

  • Game plan: exam date, what's covered, what your instructor flagged, and a study schedule
  • Notes for every chapter, with comparison tables and key terms
  • Visual summary: the diagram your study guide asks you to draw
  • Study-guide answers, hidden until you're ready to check yourself
  • Practice quiz: 40+ questions in your class's style, with explanations and hints
  • Tutor: ask anything and get answers from your own course files

Privacy in short

StudyPack has no server and no analytics. Your study pages stay in your browser, and your course materials go only to the AI you choose. Read the full policy.
